Privacy Policy

RateMate — Effective September 13, 2026

The short version: RateMate uses what you type to generate a review with AI — we never store it, never see who you are, and there are no accounts, ads, or tracking.

What the app does with your data

When you tap "Generate My Review," the app sends the venue name, venue type, your star ratings, and any optional notes you entered to our secure proxy server, which forwards them to OpenAI's API to generate a review. This data is transmitted only when you explicitly request a review.

What we do not do

  • We do not store your venue details, ratings, or notes on our servers. They are processed in memory and discarded after the review is returned.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not know who you are.
  • We do not collect your location. You type the venue name manually — the app never uses GPS or location permissions for this purpose. (Optional city/neighborhood search uses Apple's MapKit framework on-device.)
  • We do not show ads, use analytics SDKs, or track you across apps.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

What stays on your device

All venue details, ratings, and generated review text are stored only in memory while the app is open. Nothing is saved to persistent storage. Closing the app clears everything.

Third parties

OpenAI processes your venue and rating data to generate review text, subject to OpenAI's API data usage policies; API data is not used to train their models. DigitalOcean hosts our proxy server; like most hosting providers, it may keep short-lived operational logs (such as IP addresses) for security and abuse prevention. Apple's MapKit handles venue search suggestions on-device; no data is sent to Apple beyond what MapKit normally processes for search.

Children

RateMate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
